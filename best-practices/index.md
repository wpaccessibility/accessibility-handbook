# Best practices

What are the best practices for an accessible WordPress website, theme or plugin?

In addition to the [Best Practices of WordPress Core](https://make.wordpress.org/core/handbook/best-practices/) (for development) and the [WordPress Style Guide](https://make.wordpress.org/design/handbook/design-guide/) (for design), this guide will help you find information on how to make and keep your work accessible to as many people as possible.
