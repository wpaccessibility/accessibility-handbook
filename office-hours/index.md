# Office Hours

The WordPress Accessibility Team holds weekly office hours. These are an opportunity for anybody to show up and ask questions about broad accessibility issues, outside the scope of any specific WordPress ticket or issue. There is no agenda for these sessions; a member of the team will start the meeting and then simply remain available to respond if anybody asks a question.

**Currently run by** : Andrea Fercia (@afercia)

This page aims to collect discussion points and accessibility related resources emerged during the Accessibility Office Hours. For more details about the Accessibility Office Hours, please read the [kick-off post on Make WordPress](https://make.wordpress.org/accessibility/2023/09/13/accessibility-office-hours/).
